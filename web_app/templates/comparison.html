Objective Architecture Trade-off Analysis
  • Stacking Ensemble (Meta-Learning): Combines Decision Tree, Random Forest, and XGBoost using a Logistic Regression meta-learner. It achieves the highest overall accuracy (0.9994) and ties for the lowest False Negatives (FN = 46 out of 66,275 attacks), meaning it misses the fewest attacks. However, it requires maintaining all three models in memory and running two inference phases.
  • XGBoost (Gradient Boosting): Achieves the lowest False Positives of any model (FP = 4 out of 18,941 benign flows, a 99.98% specificity). If an operational environment prioritizes minimizing false alarms to prevent disrupting legitimate user sessions, XGBoost provides the cleanest benign traffic pass-through.
  • Random Forest (Bagging): 100 decorrelated trees provide high stability and robustness against traffic noise, with an accuracy of 0.9993 and FP = 9, FN = 49.
  • Voting Ensemble (Soft Voting): Averages the continuous class probability distributions of all three models, yielding balanced metrics (Accuracy: 0.9993, F1: 0.9996) without needing meta-model training.
  • Decision Tree (Baseline): Serves as the single-model baseline. While still achieving 0.9992 accuracy on this dataset, its 22 false positives are more than double the ensemble models, highlighting the variance-reduction advantage of ensembling.
